
Key Details for the 2026 Event

• Dates: November 14–15, 2026
• Format: In-person and virtual local events worldwide (plus a virtual Universal Event if no local event is nearby).
• Who Can Join: Open to all ages, skill levels, and backgrounds—no formal science or coding experience required. Teams can have up to six members.
• 2026 Challenges: Features 14 official challenges across categories like Astrophysics, Earth Science, Games, Human Exploration, Planets & Moons, Software, and Space Exploration (such as tracking Earth system trends, building junior astronaut mission trainers, and mapping Mars).

How to Participate

1. Create an Account: Sign up on the official NASA Space Apps Challenge Website.
2. Register: Register for the hackathon before or during the event weekend.
3. Join a Local Event & Form a Team: Pick a local or virtual event and build a team of up to six people.
